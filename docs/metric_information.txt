===============================================
-------------- VALUATION METRICS --------------
===============================================

peTTM -- Price-to-Earnings (Trailing 12 Months)
    Formula: Price / Earnings per Share
    Importance:
        - How much investors pay for $1 of profit
        - High P/E -> growth expectations
        - Low P/E -> possibly undervalued or declining
    
    Note:
        - Useless if earnings are negative

pb -- Price-to-Book
    Formula: Price / Book value per share
    Importance:
        - Used heavily for banks, asset-heavy firms
        - <1 can mean undervalued...or distressed
    
    Note:
        - Less useful for tech as intangibles are not captured as well

psTTM -- Price-to-Sales (Trailing 12 Months)
    Formula: Price / Revenue
    Importance: 
        - Useful when earnings are volatile or negative
        - Common for growth tech
    
    Note:
        - Revenue is harder to manipulate than earnings

pegTTM -- P/E divided by Growth
    Formula: P/E/Growth
    Importance:
        - Adjusts valuation by growth
        - PEG ~ 1 often considered 'fair'

    Note:
        - High growth companies can justify high P/E

marketCapitalization
    Formula: N/A
    Importance:
        - Company size classification (large cap, mid cap, small cap)
        - Impacts risk profile

enterpriseValue
    Formula: Market cap + debt - cash
    Importance:
        - True takeover value
        - Used in EV/EBITDA
        - Better than market cap for capital-structure comparison


===========================================
-------------- Profitability --------------
===========================================
(Does the company actually make money well)

grossMarginTTM
    Formula: Revenue - COGS
    Importance:
        - Pricing power
        - Competitive moat
    
    Note:
        - High margin -> strong business model

netProfitMarginTTM
    Formula: Net income / Revenue
    Importance:
        - True bottom-line efficiency
        - How much profit per dollar of sales

operatingMarginTTM
    Formula: Operating income / Revenue
    Importance:
        - Core business profitability
        - Strips out financing noise

roaTTM -- Return on Assets (Trailing 12 Months)
    Formula: Profit / Total Assets
    Importance:
        - Efficiency of asset usage

roeTTM -- Return on Equity (Trailing 12 Months)
    Formula: Profit / Shareholder equity
    Importance:
        - Measures stakeholder return efficiency 
    
    Note:
        - High ROE is powerful if not driven by excessive debt

roiTTM -- Return on Investment (Trailing 12 Months)
    Importance:
        - Capital allocation quality
    
    Note:
        - Broad efficiency metric
        - Often similar to ROIC

==============================================
-------------- Financial Health --------------
==============================================
(Will it survive downturns?)

currentRatioQuarterly
    Formula: Current assets / current liabilities
    Importance:
        - Short-term liquidity

quickRatioQuarterly
    Formula: (Current assets - inventory) / current liabilities
    Importance:
        - Stricter liquidity test

totalDebt/totalEquityQuarterly
    Importance:
        - Financial risk
    
    Note:
        - High debt magnifies returns AND risk

cashPerSharePerShareQuarterly
    Formula: Cash / shares outstanding
    Importance:
        - Downside protection
        - Acquisition firepower
